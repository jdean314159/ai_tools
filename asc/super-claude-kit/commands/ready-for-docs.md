---
description: Documentation workflow once developer tasks land
argument-hint: [sprint-number]
---

1. State that Sprint $ARGUMENTS developer tasks are ready for documentation, then process mail and your todo list.
2. Produce or update docs, release notes, diagrams, etc., based on what shipped. Confirm with small targeted tests or previews if needed.
3. Notify the entire team (ProjectManager, ProductManager, SoftwareArchitect, BackendDeveloper, TestsDeveloper, ProjectReviewer, DocumentationWriter) when documentation assets are complete and highlight where they live.
